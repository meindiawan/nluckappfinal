STEP 22 — PATCH studio-render.blade.php

1. Tambahkan di bagian atas Blade:
   @php($formSetting = \App\Models\ArticleFormSetting::current())

2. Pada blok form pelanggan, gunakan $formSetting untuk teks:
   - Judul: {{ $formSetting->title }}
   - Deskripsi: {{ $formSetting->description }}
   - CTA: {{ $formSetting->cta_text }}
   - consent: {{ $formSetting->consent_text }}

3. Render field secara kondisional:
   - show_name
   - show_whatsapp
   - show_email
   - show_birth_date
   - show_city
   - show_instagram

4. Required dinamis:
   email required jika require_email
   birth_date required jika require_birth_date
   city required jika require_city
   instagram required jika require_instagram
   consent required jika require_consent

Nama dan WhatsApp tetap wajib karena menjadi data inti lead.

Catatan: jangan menghapus class/design Studio yang sudah ada; hanya ubah isi/visibility field.
